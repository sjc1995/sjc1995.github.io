Hi, I'm Jiecheng Song — a Machine Learning Engineer at Meta with a Ph.D. in Statistics from Stony Brook University. My professional journey spans across leading tech companies including Meta, Amazon, and Walmart, where I've built machine learning systems for recommendation, customer behavior modeling, and natural language processing.

My research interests lie at the intersection of time series forecasting, causal inference, machine learning, natural language processing (NLP), and recommendation systems. I’ve contributed to several peer-reviewed publications in these areas, with ongoing work focused on advancing the practical impact of deep learning in real-world applications.

Beyond work, I’m passionate about skiing, exploring new places through travel, and getting lost in a good novel. Whether carving down snowy slopes or immersed in fiction, I enjoy the balance between analytical thinking and creative exploration.
